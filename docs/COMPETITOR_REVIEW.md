Before using a comparison in marketing or deciding on a provider integration,
record an accessible official page or hands-on workflow, the verification date,
free/paid availability, device/platform support and the equivalent PodyGuard
behavior. Leave unsupported cells unknown. Do not infer the absence of a feature
from an unavailable marketing page.

PodyGuard's reviewed differentiator hypothesis is the connected flow:
**plan a night → discuss expectations → form pods → play → remember → meet again**.
Scheduling and pregame agreements are new in this branch. Saved Commanders,
playgroups, result history, ratings and repeat-opponent-aware matching already
existed. No claim of market uniqueness is established by this evidence register.

For a pilot, ask three existing casual Commander groups to use invitations for
their next two nights. Record whether the organizer could share a usable invite,
guests could change RSVPs without help, the agreement avoided surprises and pod
explanations were understandable. Collect opt-in qualitative feedback through
the existing feedback flow; do not introduce tracking or public player scores.
